ScamShield India

Paste an SMS or WhatsApp message to see if it looks like a scam, and why. Works with English, Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi and Malayalam.

Screenshots are read inside your browser and are never uploaded. For best results crop the picture to just the message. Check the text it finds, fix any mistakes (symbols like ₹ are often misread), then press Check message.

Your message is analysed in memory and not stored. This is a helper, not a guarantee.
